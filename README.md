A strict momentum rider for the pump.fun paper-trading replay: it buys only coins
whose last 100 slots show at least 5 SOL of net inflow from at least 12 distinct
buyers, a price up at least 35% and within 2% of its window high, on a curve that
already holds at least 10 SOL of real reserve — with a small 0.1 SOL first stake, a
-10% stop, a -15% trailing stop armed at +20%, and a 1200-slot time limit. No adds,
no launch sniping: every variant that added into strength lost more on the dev window.
